Pawvest: Cozy Garden — Privacy Policy

Updated: November 1, 2026

1. APP AND CONTACT
Pawvest: Cozy Garden is an advertising-supported puzzle game that can also be played offline. Developer contact: kadiralk1346@gmail.com.

2. LOCAL GAME DATA
Level progress, earned leaves, settings (including language), current puzzles, hints and play statistics are saved on your device. No account or email sign-in is required. This game does not send these local saves to a Pawvest-operated server. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage can delete your progress.

3. ADVERTISING AND THIRD PARTIES
The game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for interstitial ads and optional rewarded hints, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent preferences. Google may collect IP addresses (which may approximate location), advertising/device identifiers, ad interactions, usage and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertising, protect security and prevent fraud. Please see https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

4. CONSENT AND PRIVACY CHOICES
Where required, the game requests and updates consent information through UMP before requesting ads and may display a consent form. When Google indicates it is required, a Privacy Choices control is shown in Settings. Android/Google advertising controls also provide choices. Gameplay works without advertisements, and a rewarded hint is granted only after the ad reward is confirmed.

5. INTERNET AND DISCLOSURES
Puzzles can be played offline. Ad loading and consent updates need internet access. The inspected game does not have a cloud account system or its own analytics backend. Third-party SDK behavior may vary by version and configuration. Google Play Data safety disclosures must be checked against the final published APK/AAB.

6. RETENTION AND DELETION
Game saves are retained locally until you clear app data or uninstall. Google's retention and deletion rules apply to advertising data. Google states that advertising SDK data is encrypted in transit; this does not mean all local save files are encrypted.

7. CHILDREN AND AUDIENCE
A friendly appearance alone does not mean the game targets children. The publisher must declare the intended audience in Google Play Console and comply with any applicable Families or child-directed advertising requirements.

8. UPDATES
Material changes to this policy will be published with a revised date on the public policy website.
